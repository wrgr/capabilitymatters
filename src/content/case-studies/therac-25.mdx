A therapist correcting a prescription quickly exposed a software defect. The console then showed an apparent underdose and allowed a retry, even though the patient had already received a massive overdose. The Tyler record gives us a specific question to test in simulation: what information and authority would let a therapist recognize a dangerous discrepancy, stop treatment and report it? A separate test would verify that the machine independently prevents an unsafe exposure.
