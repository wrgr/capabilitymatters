At shift change, the incoming resident must understand the patient’s condition, what to do next, and what to watch for. I-PASS gives the two clinicians a shared structure for that work: illness severity, patient summary, action list, situation awareness and contingency planning, and synthesis by the receiver. Resident practice and faculty feedback helped teams put that structure into use. Compare with [Keystone ICU](../keystone-icu-pronovost-checklist/) to see how two interventions combine learning with changes to everyday clinical work.
