  interface and the operational framing made the correct reading possible in
  principle and unsustainable in practice under combat stress.

The radar on the USS Vincennes was right: it showed Iran Air 655 climbing on its published civilian corridor. The system did not lie. But operators in the Combat Information Center, primed by a simultaneous surface battle to expect a threat, reported a Mode II military squawk the ship's own system never held and a descent its own tapes contradict, and fired, killing 290 civilians. The Fogarty Report found the Aegis system had functioned as designed, and judged that stress, task fixation, and the unconscious distortion of data — 'scenario fulfillment' — may have played a major role. Vincennes is the book's foundational human-AI-teaming case: correct performance was possible in principle and unsustainable in practice, and that gap is the engineering problem.
