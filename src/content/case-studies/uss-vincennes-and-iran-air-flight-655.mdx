The Fogarty inquiry found Aegis functioned as designed; the recorded track for Iran Air Flight 655 showed it climbing. The command team aboard USS Vincennes received reports of a descent and fired, killing all 290 people aboard. The difference between a correct track and a correct decision is the capability problem: people had to keep information tied to the right aircraft, cross-check contradictory reports and act under simultaneous combat demands.
