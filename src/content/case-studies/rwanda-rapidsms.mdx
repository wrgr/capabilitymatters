summary: >-
  Rwanda scaled RapidSMS nationally in 2013. Community health workers used
  mobile phones to register pregnancies, coordinate follow-up, and send
  emergency alerts. Ruton and colleagues analyzed routine records from 461
  health centers and compared trends in districts with and without additional
  support. Improvements appeared on selected measures in districts with added
  training, supervision, and equipment. A separate analysis by Hategeka and
  colleagues used national survey data and found no significant increase
  in the services it studied.

leInsight: >-
  A message is one step in a chain. The worker needs to recognize when to
  refer, the facility needs to act, and the family needs a way to reach care.

  Map who sends, receives, and acts on a referral. Design a practice scenario

Start with the referral. A community health worker may recognize that someone needs care and send the right message. Whether the family receives care also depends on who sees that message, what help the facility can provide, and whether the family can reach it. RapidSMS makes those dependencies visible.
