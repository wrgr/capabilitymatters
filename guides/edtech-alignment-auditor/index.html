Explain how a design could help people select and configure technology that elicits the intended learner thinking, and what evidence would justify another iteration.

Students can be highly fluent with devices and learning apps while the activity itself remains weakly connected to the intended lesson. Schools often evaluate tools by engagement, usability, or vendor claims rather than by the learner actions and evidence the tool produces.

Before you begin

Begin with the use case below. Keep the proposed design separate from what the current exercise actually implements.

A use case to work with

A literacy app asks learners to tap vocabulary definitions. The auditor identifies that the lesson target is inferential reading and proposes a short evidence-based explanation task before the app is used.

Try this

  1. Read the representative use case. Identify who must do what and under which conditions.
  2. Try the available exercise with a fictional example. Record your first decision and one revision.
  3. Choose one assumption to test. Use the evidence prompts in the brief to define an outcome that could change your mind.

Leave with

Keep the evidence in view

Working teaching exercise; the broader design and its learning effects remain hypotheses.

Evidence prompts from the brief

Predefine evidence: objective-task alignment, quality of learner evidence, teacher decision quality, student performance, and tool abandonment. A key disconfirming signal is: teachers produce better-looking lesson plans but learner actions and outcomes remain unchanged.

Help me make sense of this
  1. Separate what happened from why. Your observation is the result; your explanation is a claim that may need testing.
  2. Check another explanation. What else could account for the result? What comparison would distinguish them?
  3. Try without the support. Could you explain or perform the task in a new situation? Identify what evidence you would need.
  4. Choose one next move. Return to the concept, compare another example, or test one changed assumption.
