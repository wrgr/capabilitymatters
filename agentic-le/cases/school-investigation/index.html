Evidence Studio: investigate, test and reconsider

About this resource · How to use it · Learning engineering connections

What is this resource?

A school-investigation candidate for examining science reasoning and how an inquiry task is supported. This is a candidate product; affected-person review and field evidence remain pending.

How should I use it?

  1. Work through the candidate’s investigation prompts, inspect its feedback and compare the requirements with the original school-science run.
  2. Open the requirements and manifest, then follow Replay this case to the design decisions. The downloadable bundle preserves the original record.

How does this connect to LENS and learning engineering?

Learning engineering makes the intended reasoning observable while preserving the evidence needed to assess an independent explanation. Technical execution does not establish a learning benefit.
