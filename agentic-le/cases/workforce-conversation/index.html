Conversation Lab: facts, perspectives and next moves

About this resource · How to use it · Learning engineering connections

What is this resource?

A fictional team-lead conversation rehearsal with authored openings, fact/explanation separation and a change in protected time and coverage. This is a candidate product; affected-person review and field evidence remain pending.

How should I use it?

  1. Compare openings, separate observations from explanations, then reconsider the proposal when time and coverage are withdrawn.
  2. Open the requirements and manifest, then follow Replay this case to the design decisions. The downloadable bundle preserves the original record.

How does this connect to LENS and learning engineering?

Communication practice cannot supply protected time, staffing or agreement. LENS requires these conditions to remain distinct from a learner’s conversational skill. Technical execution does not establish a learning benefit.
