Lifecycle role contract

Inherit responsibilities. Demonstrate proficiency.

A role profile defines a bounded contribution to the project. A parent supplies shared duties; a specialist or domain profile adds context and requested proficiency. Inheritance supplies no performance evidence.

Inspect the resolved role contract

Registry 1.1.0; framework 1.0.0. The domain names come from Capability Matters’ public LENS framework. These proficiency descriptors are proposed simulation expectations, not an adopted credentialing standard or a statement of human or model proficiency.

Requested contribution levels

Awareness

Identify the relevant issue, uncertainty and need for specialist or human review.

Evidence needed to review performance:

  • Name a constraint in context
  • Identify the limit of the available evidence

Applied contribution

Produce a bounded task artifact, compare explanations and revise it against a counterexample.

Evidence needed to review performance:

  • A task artifact with a stated criterion
  • Traceable supporting and conflicting evidence
  • Revision after a changed condition

Integrative orchestration

Integrate specialist work, preserve dissent, reconcile human/system requirements and choose an accountable next action.

Evidence needed to review performance:

  • A decision linking specialist contributions
  • Explicit tradeoffs and unresolved dissent
  • A justified return to the earliest affected stage

Resolved role profiles

Shared contributor → specialist → domain specialization is a composition path, not an automatic promotion. Duties accumulate; child targets replace the parent target for the same domain. Human approval boundaries remain in code and do not expand through role inheritance.

Learning engineer

contributor → orchestrator

Integrate learning sciences, human-centered design, systems requirements and evidence. Preserve dissent and choose the earliest stage to revisit.

Inherited and specialized duties
  • Distinguish supplied facts, assumptions, hypothetical events and missing evidence.
  • State human and system capability requirements; examine agency, dignity, access and workload.
  • Preserve dissent, correction, non-AI alternatives and accountable human gates.
  • Synthesize drafts into a usable stage artifact and explicit decision.

School science domain specialist

contributor → specialist → domain → school-science-domain

Examine reasoning from conflicting fictional evidence, classroom participation, teacher facilitation and access. Separate explanation from grading and require teacher, student and caregiver review before real use.

Inherited and specialized duties
  • Distinguish supplied facts, assumptions, hypothetical events and missing evidence.
  • State human and system capability requirements; examine agency, dignity, access and workload.
  • Preserve dissent, correction, non-AI alternatives and accountable human gates.
  • Challenge the plan within your discipline and propose a reviewable artifact; do not supply human approval.
  • Tie the contribution to the current seed and actual earlier journal events.
  • Use only supplied fictional evidence; do not infer student ability from reading fluency or tool completion.
  • Consider multilingual access, shared devices, discussion norms and teacher workload alongside instructional needs.

Adding a domain or future competency framework

Extend an existing concrete profile in the versioned registry, name the domain assumptions and required practitioner validation, then test resolution. Future runs store the exact resolved profiles and framework hashes. A new authoritative framework can be added with source and version identifiers, an explicit crosswalk and separately reviewed proficiency evidence; a name match or successful model response does not establish equivalence.
