About this resource · How to use it · Learning engineering connections

What is this resource?

The preserved earlier fixed-workflow pilot: separate rubric indices, browser coverage, missing evidence and retained Build prototypes. These results do not evaluate the revised dynamic-conversation protocol. No human outcomes were measured.

How should I use it?

  1. Compare each of the three axes separately. Read the known/planned counts, standard deviations, and unknown values before interpreting a condition mean.
  2. Open a repetition. Included candidates show two reviewer indices, browser-check states, an exact source fingerprint, and an isolated preview. Excluded candidates retain the reason and have no evaluated preview.
  3. Read the paper and provenance notes for the controlled contrasts and evidence limits. Observed browser flows and model ratings do not establish human learning or field impact.

How does this connect to LENS and learning engineering?

The study makes expertise assignments, shared understanding, and work allocation inspectable across the LENS capability cycle. The flower organizes contributing domains; human flourishing support, system performance, and learning remain distinct evidence questions.

How the indices and reviewer pairs work

Each review rates nine criteria, three on each axis. An axis index is twenty-five times the mean of its three 0–4 ratings. The displayed candidate value is the mean of two complete reviewer indices. A missing criterion makes that reviewer’s axis unknown; a missing reviewer index makes the candidate axis unknown.

Two fresh calls to the same model (gpt-6.1-sol, reasoning effort low) produced the reviewer pair. These are repeated model judgments, not independent human raters. Failed review attempts remain in the record as unknown values. Exact evidence quotations support traceability, not the correctness of a judgment.

Limits on interpretation

The evidence concerns one fictional scenario, the configured model and instructions, and a small set of generated candidates. Descriptive differences can inform further investigation; they do not establish causal effects in human teams or a population-level advantage.

The flower’s eight domains organize starting responsibilities around shared understanding. They are not an exhaustive taxonomy or a validated measurement of anyone’s expertise. Human flourishing support, system performance, and learning remain distinct views of the available design evidence.

The earlier paper is preserved in the offline historical appendix. Read the newer paper about dynamic conversations and scenario tests.

Provenance and open evidence gaps

  • Primary journal hash chains verified for 15 of 15 executed runs.
  • Generation retained 588 accepted responses from 588 requests; 0 were rejected and 0 remain unresolved.
  • Recorded instruction checks: 0 accepted-response schema violations, 0 prompt-assignment violations, and 0 calls with provenance errors.
  • Complete provider receipts cover 560 of 588 terminal generation calls. 28 calls have receipt gaps; available totals do not establish complete token or tool-use evidence for those calls.
  • Separately retained raw event-log copies have discrepancies for 5 runs. Original records are preserved without repair.
  • Model review retained 26 accepted and 2 failed attempts. Invalid attempts remain unknown; no replacement rating is selected.
