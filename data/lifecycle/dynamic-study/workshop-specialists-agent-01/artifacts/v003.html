2. Explain your plan

Connect your proposed activity, resources, timing, assistance and supplied participant provisions to the relevant facts and constraints. Explain a fallback and its trigger, or the clarification you need. If the supplied facts resolve the relevant uncertainty, you can say why.

A possible structure: I propose … because the case says …; if … changes, I would … because …; I still need to know … .
