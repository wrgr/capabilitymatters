1. Read the case

Observation and assumption

An observation is a specific detail supported by the supplied facts. An assumption adds an explanation or motive the facts do not establish. An event alone does not tell you why someone acted.

Different questions and next steps can be reasonable. Use the applied case to decide.

2. Try a response

A few sentences are enough to start. Include a supported observation, one useful clarification, and a possible next step. There is no score or required script.

Feedback and revision

This is a guided review, not an assessment. Optional text hints check a small set of English phrases and question marks. They can misread quotations or negation and miss paraphrases or other languages. They do not verify evidence, meaning, tone, or feasibility. A missing hint does not mean a response is sound.

Feedback will appear here after you try a response. You can skip without writing anything.
