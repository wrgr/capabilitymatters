Controls and workstations explained

These controls apply to the full-shift simulation. Guided practice and the scripted demo have their own step and playback controls.

Start shift or Next decision
Advance until the next manager decision or the end of the shift.
Run and Pause
Run advances simulated time automatically; Pause stops automatic advancement so you can inspect the situation.
Plus one minute
Advance exactly one simulated minute.
Slow Normal Fast
Change playback speed, not the simulated task durations or shift length.
Pause at decisions
Stop automatic playback when a decision needs attention. You can inspect evidence before continuing.
Receiving
Accept incoming orders or materials and check them into the workflow.
Pick and Label
Select the requested items and prepare their identifying labels.
Dispatch
Send completed work onward and compare delivery with the promised time. The three stations form the fictional work sequence; they are not learning-engineering pipeline stages.

Use Tab to reach controls, Enter or Space to activate a button, and the arrow keys to change a focused select. Outcomes are teaching-model results, not validated operational predictions.
