The Capability Pipeline

Five worked cases with why / teaching points. Study, Find the turn, or Predict first; source narratives retained, with historical claims not independently rechecked.

About this resource · How to use it · Learning engineering connections

What is this resource?

A paired resource: five worked systems cases and an editable branching simulation organized around Understand, Map, Design, Build, Instrument, Deploy, Evaluate and Refine.

How should I use it?

  1. Choose Explore five cases to study a case, find its turning point, or predict before revealing the explanation.
  2. Choose Choose your own adventure to make a strategy decision, inspect the outcomes and compare a revised branch. The direct links below open each view.
  3. Explain which evidence would justify returning to an earlier stage. Treat the scenario probabilities as editable teaching assumptions.

How does this connect to LENS and learning engineering?

LENS treats capability as an interaction between people and the conditions for action. The cycle connects problem framing, design and measurement: an upstream choice determines what later evaluation can observe. Revisit assumptions when results conflict with a plan rather than treating the eight stages as a completion checklist.
