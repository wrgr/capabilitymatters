Blind evidence review

About this resource · How to use it · Learning engineering connections

What is this resource?

An isolated evidence review workspace for scoring opaque learning engineering candidates before revealing team assignments. It preserves task-level human flourishing support, system performance, and learning evidence as three separate axes.

How should I use it?

  1. Import an opaque packet or bundle. Inspect the task, constraints, rubric, and evidence. Full-solution packets separate executable Build, generated stage records, and observations; proposed repairs receive no implementation credit. Keep historical artifact-only reviews in their original scope.
  2. For each criterion, record a supported rating, plausible lower and upper bounds, rationale, and an exact evidence quotation. Keep insufficient evidence unknown and describe any identity clues in the limitations.
  3. Inspect the probability assumptions, export a draft if needed, then freeze the review with the local runner. Export the frozen record to the organizer for comparison and identity reveal after the selected reviews are frozen.

How does this connect to LENS and learning engineering?

Learning engineering connects designed contributions to evidence about what learners and systems can do. LENS also asks whether people have agency, access, and viable conditions for action. Withholding assignment labels helps test the artifact rather than reward the intended expertise, while modeled uncertainty makes incomplete evidence and contextual assumptions visible. These task-level indices do not establish observed learning gains or human flourishing.
