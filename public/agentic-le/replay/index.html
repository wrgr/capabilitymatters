Who contributes, and what they can claim
The learning engineer integrates the work. Specialist agents contribute project-specific expertise; they do not replace practitioners or affected people. Scenario events are hypothetical. The transcript contains public deliberation, not private model reasoning.

Practice a difficult conversation
Use a fictional case. Your notes stay in this page unless you export them. This tool checks structure; it does not assess competence.

      
Introduce a counterexample
The handoff deadline was changed in a channel your colleague could not access.
Write your own plan before reviewing the structure. A colleague-led role-play is the next step for testing listening and judgment.
What counts as evidence?
Observe how you listen, check an inference, revise under disagreement and choose a feasible action in a role-play. A completed form or polished script does not establish transfer to work.
