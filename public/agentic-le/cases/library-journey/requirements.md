# Design

## Problem and people

Access Studio: choose a route, keep control is a fictional community-event participation exercise. The task is to explore an invented notice, inspect a participation condition and choose an acceptable next step. Independent online completion is not the objective. An unresolved choice, conditional assistance, paper preference, passing or stopping is legitimate.

Relevant people are adult patrons with varied reading, languages and device access; librarians, volunteers, service owners and accessibility practitioners. None participates in this rehearsal. This is a provisional Design revision, not an approved requirement set.

Sequence 18 supplies one hypothetical event: prepared packets run out, and a fictional patron objects that viewing cards on a screen does not provide the desired paper route. This is authored scenario data, not a participant quotation or service incident. It invalidates the delivery-equivalence wording in sequence 15. Revise Design only: the Understand framing and Map separation of preference from provision remain applicable, with their human gates unresolved. Sequence 2 records parent preservation, not successful verification.

Rival explanations for difficulty include unclear notices, unnecessary account requirements, language or format mismatch, inaccessible controls, interrupted device time and absent assistance. Rejecting a medium may express ordinary preference. Apparent ease could reflect familiarity, repeated controls or facilitator prompting. Neither pattern establishes a learning need.

## Human and system capability

Human requirements: patrons control participation, support preferences and whether a description represents their choices. Selecting, pointing or speaking may express a decision; silence is not agreement. Passing and stopping require no explanation. An actually available facilitator may read authored information or restate selections with permission, but may not select an answer, infer understanding or promise service assistance.

Service owners control actual participation requirements and accepted routes. Assistance providers control offered support. An accountable materials provider must establish complete usable packets independently of the candidate before offering a facilitated paper exercise. Librarians must examine preparation, supply, formats, language arrangements, shared-device handling and workload. No provider, supplies or support capacity is presumed available.

System requirements: distinguish explicit preference, authored example conditions, unknown real provision and next action. For paper, separately represent content available to view, fictional physical supply, unknown real packet availability, usable format and service acceptance. Fixed local rules suffice; no AI inference is needed. The candidate cannot register, send requests, create accounts, arrange printing, replenish supplies, determine eligibility or judge capability.

Use no personal, credential or account fields, open text, scores, deficit labels, network, storage, telemetry, external assets, links or real submissions. Keep only the current draft, optional initial snapshot and temporary editing/activity state. A choice cannot create staff capacity, a physical packet or acceptance by a service.

## Experience and decisions

Opening mission: “Community Drawing Hour is invented. Explore its example notice and choose a possible next step. This exercise cannot register anyone. Use fictional choices only.” The invented notice offers morning and afternoon sessions, says materials are provided in this example and asks patrons to “Confirm your place.” Session preference is optional and never a reservation.

A small illustrated route board has complete text labels: Explore the event, Choose a route, Check a condition, Review your plan. Illustration conveys no additional required information. One route choice reveals its consequence without automatic advancement. No completed worksheet, baseline or second task is required.

Offer mock online practice, conditional staff explanation and a paper preference, alongside No route works for me and Leave undecided. Pause, Decline and Stop remain equally reachable activity choices. No route is preselected. Longer detail is optional; essential fiction, provision and control statements stay visible when detail is closed.

Online practice reveals: “This invented portal includes an account step before confirmation. No account is created here.” Local controls inspect that requirement, seek an explanation if assistance exists, reconsider or stop. Staff explanation yields: “Your draft seeks an explanation if suitable assistance exists. Real availability and language support are unknown. No request is sent.” Neither route contains account or contact fields.

Paper preference reveals two distinct actions. “View the paper materials” opens the complete authored packet without changing the chosen route or its delivery condition. “Use a prepared paper packet” expresses a preferred next action conditional on a complete usable packet actually being supplied. It does not confirm possession, create a packet or start a service process. Show beside both: “Real packet availability, usable format and service acceptance are unknown. Viewing these materials does not provide physical paper.” Viewing is optional; a person rejecting the screen need not inspect it.

Offer optional authored changes through short controls, without requiring every scenario. The existing change imagines staff temporarily unavailable. The new change from sequence 18 imagines prepared packets exhausted. Its paper consequence reads: “In this example, prepared packets have run out. The materials can be viewed here, but a physical paper route is unresolved. No printing or replenishment is arranged.” Preserve paper preference and offer keeping it unresolved, another route, undecided, pause or stop. Never substitute screen practice. Neither hypothetical condition changes unknown real provision.

The current plan is always reviewable. Optional “Keep this as my initial plan” captures explicit choices and their condition context; otherwise show “Initial plan not recorded.” Support and fallback are optional. “None selected” never means “No support needed.” Output describes route, next action, support preference, delivery/assistance dependencies, controlling party and fallback. Keep “Fictional plan; no registration or request sent” adjacent to it.

Before/Now comparison is optional. “Correct initial plan” edits initial choices within their original condition context; “Change current plan” edits current choices. Identify the side being edited. Commit replaces dependent wording; Cancel preserves committed state. Viewing materials cannot remove an exhausted-supply condition, fabricate a baseline or rewrite the initial context. Feedback describes selections and unresolved conditions without praise, recommendations or capability judgments.

Optional Neighborhood Story Circle has its own notice: “Check joining arrangements.” Arrangements remain unresolved; no account requirement is stated. Its authored change imagines shared-computer time ending, which pauses activity without changing preference. Use fewer initial suggestions while retaining Help, paper content, correction, refusal and stopping. Task replacement explains removal of the previous draft and offers Cancel. No Drawing Hour condition is silently inherited.

Cover, Clear and Stop precede task controls in keyboard order. Cover conceals all choice-bearing content, pending edits and announcements, moves focus to neutral status and pauses active practice; stopped practice stays stopped. Reveal changes visibility only. Resume is separate. Confirmed Clear removes task choices, snapshots, scenario selections, pending edits and derived messages, preserves visibility/activity status and creates no new draft. Cancel clearing preserves content. A fresh undecided draft requires explicit action. Reload restores no prior choices. Clearing concerns candidate-held content, not device-wide records.

## Requirements and rationale

R1 — Honest dependencies and authority. Origins: Public library specialist, sequence 5, D1.problem/D5.context; Systems engineer, sequence 7, D1.coupling; Responsible AI specialist, sequence 13, D3.delegation. Pair each preference with its authored condition, controlling party and unknown real provision. Human reason: a preference is not another party’s commitment. System reason: route selection must not alter assistance or material availability. Check: choose staff explanation and apply fictional unavailability; preference persists, assistance is unresolved, no request is confirmed and alternatives remain reachable. Actual provision is unknown.

R2 — Physical paper delivery. Origins: integrated R7 at sequence 15 and scenario at sequence 18, D1.coupling/D3.agency/D4.verification; paper proposals at sequences 5 and 7. Separate viewing content from intending to use independently prepared paper. Human reason: choosing paper does not imply acceptance of screen use. System reason: content visibility cannot satisfy delivery. Check: choose paper, record an initial plan if wanted, apply exhausted supplies and view the packet; preference persists, physical provision stays unresolved and no printing or replacement medium is promised. Separately give a reviewer the actual complete packet to conduct the task without a screen. Without that packet, the delivery check is unmet. Usable formats and human readiness remain pending.

R3 — Manageable practice and descriptive feedback. Origins: Human-centered designer, sequence 9, D2.prototyping/D5.context; Learning scientist, sequence 11, D2.practice. One choice reveals a concrete consequence; all additional selectors, snapshots and changes remain optional. Human reason: participation must not require a finished plan. System reason: inspection is a coherent task without mandatory collection. Check: inspect the account condition, leave the next action undecided and stop without validation errors or completion judgments. Patron review must establish acceptable burden and wording.

R4 — Contestable synthesis and faithful correction. Origins: Systems engineer, sequence 7, D4.verification; designer, sequence 9, D3.agency; Responsible AI specialist, sequence 13, D3.delegation; scenario, sequence 18. Derive each plan from explicit choices and its own condition context, with separate initial/current correction and cancellation. Human reason: readable output must remain rejectable. System reason: edits must remove stale dependent claims without accumulating history. Check: record paper before supply exhaustion, apply exhaustion, change current route, correct the initial preference to undecided and cancel another edit. Each side must retain its own conditions and exact committed choices; viewing content must not repair supply. Comparison comprehension is untested.

R5 — Access, privacy and recovery. Origins: Systems engineer, sequence 7, D1.coupling/D4.verification; designer, sequence 9, D2.prototyping/D5.context; Responsible AI specialist, sequence 13, D5.privacy; preserved controls at sequence 2. Provide plain labels, text alternatives, responsive reflow, visible focus and predictable focus restoration. Separate visibility, activity and content presence. Human reason: interruption must preserve exposure and continuation control. System reason: hiding one summary cannot conceal every representation. Check cover/reveal/resume, cancel/clear, stopping and reload from comparison, supply-change and practice states; inspect keyboard and assistive-technology access, announcements and stale content. Also inspect absence of prohibited data collection, storage and external activity. No checks or accessibility certification exist.

R6 — Practice and evidence boundaries. Origins: Learning scientist, sequence 11, D4.evidence/D4.transfer; practice proposals at sequences 5, 7 and 9. Story Circle has its own unresolved notice and device-time change; help remains reachable. Human reason: immediate supported performance is not demonstrated transfer. System reason: task-specific data and conditions must not leak across replacement. Check entry, requested detail, cancellation, unresolved arrangements and stopping; no inherited account requirement, reward, score or independence claim appears. Later retention/adaptation requires a separate reviewed task, timing and documented support conditions.

R7 — Human readiness and alternatives. Origins: domain specialist, sequence 5, D5.context; Responsible AI specialist, sequence 13, D3.delegation/D5.privacy; scenario, sequence 18. Make complete packet content inspectable before digital participation, but describe screen-independent use only as conditional on separate provision. Human reason: refusal, format choice and support demand require accountable arrangements. System reason: the candidate cannot approve readiness. Check that reviewers can distinguish preference, viewing and physical use; a prepared packet must support the full decision without the screen. Patron, librarian, service-owner and practitioner review must establish relevance, handling, formats and sustainable workload before later use. No operational approval is supplied.

## Tradeoffs and alternatives

Local dependency statements add reading but prevent hidden assumptions. Progressive detail may reduce burden while obscuring conditions, so essential limits stay visible. Optional comparison enables inspectable revision but adds state, privacy exposure and memory demands; review may remove it. Fixed choices avoid personal disclosure but omit possibilities, so unlisted and undecided outcomes remain valid.

Retain dissent: clearer notices, verified route information or removal of unnecessary account friction may solve the problem directly. A separately prepared static route board and packet are non-AI options. Conversation depends on an actually available person. Reviewers may choose these alternatives or stop development. Digital packet inspection alone is not equivalent offline delivery.

## Acceptance checks

R1–R7 define proposed observable checks; none has run. Intended quality is a voluntary, manageable and correctable decision with honest dependencies. No implemented candidate or observed technical behavior is supplied here.

Technical verification must exercise correction, cancellation, replacement, concealment, clearing and reload, including focus, announcements and narrow/magnified layouts. Physical delivery verification separately requires the prepared packet; screen inspection cannot pass it. Passing any check establishes only the exercised behavior.

Human interaction review must examine wantedness, comprehension, refusal, language/format arrangements, privacy discoverability and provider burden. Learning, retention, transfer and flourishing require separate inquiry. Completion, speed, route revision or fewer prompts establish none of them.
