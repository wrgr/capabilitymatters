Access Studio: choose a route, keep control

Runnable candidate product. The bundle includes the executable tool, requirements, versioned artifacts, project journal and handoff manifest. Human review and field evidence remain pending.

About this resource · How to use it · Learning engineering connections

What is this resource?

Access Studio: a public-library service journey candidate in which a visitor chooses a route and retains control. This is a candidate product; affected-person review and field evidence remain pending.

How should I use it?

  1. Compare the available routes and what each requires, then inspect how choices, assistance and changing conditions affect participation.
  2. Open the requirements and manifest, then follow Replay this case to the design decisions. The downloadable bundle preserves the original record.

How does this connect to LENS and learning engineering?

Human-centered design must preserve agency when access conditions differ. In LENS, a successful route and an opportunity to decline are part of the system conditions for valued participation. Technical execution does not establish a learning benefit.
