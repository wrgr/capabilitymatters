About this resource · How to use it · Learning engineering connections

What is this resource?

A browser-local collection of the reflections you have chosen to save while exploring Capability Matters resources.

How should I use it?

  1. Save a reflection from a learning guide if you want it to appear here. Saving is optional.
  2. Read your observations alongside their explanations, download a copy, or clear saved reflections using the notebook controls.

How does this connect to LENS and learning engineering?

Reflection supports the habit of separating observations, explanations and proposed next tests. A saved note can help revisit an assumption; neither the number of notes nor a confident explanation establishes learning, independent capability or intervention impact.

  • LENS: capabilities and conditions

Revisit your observations, compare your explanations, and choose a next test. Saved reflections are notes for your own use; they are not assessed capability or course completion.

Only reflections you chose to save in this browser appear here. They are not sent to a server. Download a copy if you want to keep them.
