Explore what evidence can tell us →
Completion records

Completion shows participation. To assess readiness, also observe people doing representative work under the expected conditions.

Performance on representative work

Observe the work, including exceptions and recovery. Record the tools, help and conditions so the capability claim matches the evidence.

A confidence survey

Confidence can reveal concerns and guide support. Compare it with observed performance before using it as evidence of readiness.

Human capability belongs in the design.

Hardware gets reliability analysis. Software gets tested to specification. Human capability needs the same attention, from the first design decisions through operation and revision.

Capability is a systems engineering problem.

How people develop, retain, and transfer operational competence is as tractable as any other engineering domain—when you bring the right methods, the right evidence, and the right integration into the system lifecycle.

Decision-grade evidence. Not opinions about training.

Can you prove your people are ready? Not with completion rates or satisfaction scores. With evidence that links learning interventions to operational outcomes—evidence rigorous enough to drive design decisions.

Three foundations, one shared purpose

The human, the science and the system must succeed together.

Develop capability that serves people, rests on credible learning evidence, and works in the conditions where it matters.

HumanHuman flourishing
People have a say, find purpose, and can act with dignity and security. Examine who benefits and who carries the cost.
Does this help people live and act well?
ScienceLearning sciences
Use research about learning, practice and transfer. Choose measures and comparisons that can test the explanation, including evidence that could challenge it.
What supports the claim that learning occurred?
SystemSystems engineering
Connect people, tools, tasks, authority and feedback. Test the complete arrangement, including its limits, maintenance and recovery.
Can this work reliably in the intended setting?

Our synthesis connects these fields. OECD’s Education for Human Flourishing (2025) informs the human-purpose component. Its Learning Compass also connects agency with individual and collective well-being.

Learning engineering in practice

The right tools, working together.

Learning engineering brings complementary methods and expertise together around a capability challenge. Skills can span domains, and these examples represent only some of the fields and forms of expertise that can contribute.

  • Learning design

    Shape practice, feedback and support around the work people need to do.

  • Learning sciences & research

    Draw on evidence about how people learn, retain and transfer what they know.

  • AI & emerging technology

    Test how new technology can support learning and work.

  • Data & learning analytics

    Make progress, capability gaps and consequences visible.

  • Human–machine teaming

    Define roles, oversight and recovery when people and technology work together.

  • Systems engineering & design thinking

    Connect requirements, people, tools and workflows; test assumptions in context.

  • Communication & leadership

    Build shared understanding and coordinate decisions across a team.

  • Ethics & compliance

    Examine rights, access, responsibilities and constraints throughout the work.

  • Domain application

    Bring the realities of the job and the experience of the people affected.

What we mean by capability

The capacity of a person, team, or human-system arrangement to accomplish valued work under stated conditions, with usable opportunities to act.
