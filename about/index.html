About this site

About Capability Matters

Capability Matters connects learning engineering to what people need to do in complex systems. It supports Johns Hopkins’ Learning Engineering for Next-Generation Systems (LENS) specialization through case studies, interactive experiments, and prototype design work.

Find what you need

  • Case Studies: documented examples, sources, and limits on interpretation. Includes the draft casebook.
  • Experiments: learning labs, research projects, and creative demonstrations you can explore.
  • Prototypes: capability gaps, design briefs, available tools, and plans to evaluate them.
  • Show Your Work: a LENS community paper on capability, coupled learner and system performance, and human flourishing.

For the wider field’s reading lists, tools, events, and community, visit the Learning Engineering Commons and IEEE ICICLE.

Where it applies

LENS focuses on high-consequence, high-impact domains — where human capability is inseparable from system performance and the cost of getting it wrong is measured in lives, readiness, and equity. Seven recur across the program's case material and teaching: healthcare, defense & national security, education at scale, aviation, energy, manufacturing & industrial, and government & public infrastructure.

The LENS framework

LENS reads every case through five competency domains. Each case study on this site is tagged to the domain(s) it exercises, so the same framework runs from a hospital to a flight deck to a public-sector IT migration:

  1. Systems Analysis
  2. Iterative Development
  3. Human-System Collaboration
  4. Test & Evaluation
  5. Sociotechnical Constraints

People & programs

LENS is a concentration within the JHU MEd in Learning Design and Technology, jointly run by the Johns Hopkins School of Education and the Applied Physics Laboratory, with an IEEE ICICLE partnership. Faculty in the concentration include James Diamond and William Gray-Roncal (School of Education / APL). Full profiles for practitioners across the wider field live at lecommons's community pages.

For Will Gray-Roncal’s other writing and projects, visit his personal site.

Scope & caveats

  • LENS-focused, not comprehensive — on purpose. This site stays narrow; it doesn't try to be a complete index of the field. For that, see lecommons.
  • ICICLE is authoritative. When ICICLE and this site differ on a definition or taxonomy, defer to ICICLE. This site doesn't claim to speak for IEEE ICICLE or host its content — it connects to it.
  • Links may rot. External resources change. File an issue if you find one.
